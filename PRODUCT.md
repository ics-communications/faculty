# PRODUCT.md — ICS Faculty site

`faculty.icscanada.edu` — the public faculty directory of the **Institute for Christian
Studies**, a graduate school in Toronto (founded 1967) offering MA, MWS and PhD degrees in
philosophy in the Reformational tradition.

## What this site is

A rebuild and redesign of the existing Google Sites faculty directory. It lists everyone
who teaches or has taught at ICS, in five standings, and gives each current scholar a full
page of their own work.

| Standing | Who |
|---|---|
| Senior Members | The six salaried ICS professors |
| Cross-Appointed | Two professors shared with The King's University, Edmonton |
| Emeriti | Seven retired or deceased professors, with years of service |
| Adjuncts | Three scholars who supervise and teach without a salaried post |
| Sessionals | Three scholars teaching individual courses |

## Who it is for

1. **Prospective graduate students** deciding whether there is anyone here they want to
   study under. They arrive from `icscanada.edu/academics` and read for fit, not credentials.
2. **Academic peers** checking a citation, a publication list, or a CV.
3. **The ICS community** — members, donors, alumni — following the people they support.

## Visitor mode: Read

The visitor comes to understand *who thinks about what here*. Comprehension and wayfinding
come first; the reading experience then has to be worth staying inside. This is not a
conversion surface — admissions lives on `icscanada.edu`.

## Product truth

- **The distinctive asset is the epigraph.** Every senior member chose a quotation to sit at
  the top of their page under the heading "Something Worth Considering…". Those nine
  quotations — Husserl, Adorno, Rahner, Illich, Arnold, Krier, Palmer, Vollenhoven,
  Wittgenstein — are the truest, least copyable thing on the site. They say what kind of
  school this is faster than any prose block could.
- Bio content is the faculty's own writing, carried over verbatim from the old site. It is
  not ours to rewrite.
- Publication lists are long and unevenly formatted. That is normal for the genre and must
  stay readable rather than be truncated.
- Not everyone has every section. Adjuncts and sessionals have short entries; emeriti have
  archives, not research programmes.

## Constraints (given by the user, not inferred)

- The **colour scheme comes from the ICS design references** (`info/Design-References/`) —
  wine, burnt copper, cream, Libre Baskerville + Outfit.
- The **navigation bar alone** is black / white with red accents drawn from the ICS logos.
- The **footer is copied exactly** from the ICS main site.
- **One unified stylesheet** across every page.
- **Mobile-optimized.**
- Static HTML. No build step at serve time, no framework, no analytics.
