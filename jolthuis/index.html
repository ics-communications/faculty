Emeritus Faculty  /  James Olthuis

James Olthuis

PhD

  • Emeritus Professor of Philosophical Theology
jimo@icscanada.edu

Joined the faculty in 1968 and has practised as a psychotherapist since 1982. In I Pledge You My Troth and The Beautiful Risk he trades mastery for love, and reads relationship itself as the site of healing.
